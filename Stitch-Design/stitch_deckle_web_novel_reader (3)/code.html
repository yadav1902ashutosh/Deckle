menu_book Tome Synopsis

Born carrying nine lives of karmic misfortune, the infant Xia Yi was destined for an early funeral before his ninth birthday. To preserve his fragile spirit thread, his grandfather arranged an unconventional marriage with a mysterious ghost child-bride from the subterranean depths of the ancestral cemetery.

Inheriting an antique Yin-Yang Taoist manual bound in bleached vellum, Xia Yi unlocks the forbidden art of commanding ethereal wraiths, celestial ancestors, and divine retribution lightning. From an abandoned county hamlet besieged by vengeful apparitions to the sovereign imperial courts of the Nine Heavens, he carves his mortal bloodline into the annals of godhood.

“When heavenly Dao abandons righteousness, the tribulation falls upon the gods themselves.”
