Chapter 9568: Garrison (駐兵)

chevron_right

menu_book Synopsis

Unabridged Archive

Even before I was born, I was cursed. Possessing all five yin elements, I was born to attract vengeful ghosts. A knowledgeable fortune teller said I wouldn't live past seven, and after death, I was destined to be raised as a blood-clothed ghost to bring calamity upon the world. To save my life, my grandmother arranged a child bride for me, giving me a semblance of peace, though I later uncovered a harrowing truth—my bride wasn't human... Accustomed to enduring a fragile existence, I swallowed every misfortune, until the tragic morning my grandmother was murdered. Disregarding karmic retribution and heaven's decree, I inherited her ancient mantle of raising spirits, stepping into the realm of yin and yang to sever the karmic strings of fate itself.
