The Web Serial "Story Arc" Model Explained

Why Book is the anchor, Volume is an optional arc, and Chapter numbering is continuous.

ANCHOR

The Book Entity

In serialized web fiction (Royal Road, Wuxiaworld), readers search for Novels, not isolated volumes. The Book holds all 5-star ratings, total view counts, user reviews, and catalog cards.

Prevents rating & review dilution across arcs.
OPTIONAL

The Volume (Arc)

Volumes are story partitions (e.g. The Clown Arc, The Academy Arc). If an author defines 0 volumes, chapters render flat with zero friction. If volumes exist, the Table of Contents automatically groups them.

volume_id is nullable on chapters table.
CONTINUOUS

Continuous Numbering

Volume 1 ends at Chapter 40; Volume 2 starts at Chapter 41. This allows unbreakable permalinks like /read/45, seamless auto-next chapter loading, and simple bookmarks.
